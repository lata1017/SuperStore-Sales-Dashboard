# SuperStore Sales Analysis & Forecasting Dashboard

## 📊 Executive Summary

This project features a comprehensive data visualization suite designed to analyze the retail performance of a North American "SuperStore". By synthesizing transactional data from **2019 and 2020**, the dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue streams, regional profitability, and logistics efficiency.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard monitors four critical business metrics to evaluate overall health:

* 
**Total Revenue:** 1.57M in aggregate sales.


* 
**Net Profit:** 175.26K.


* 
**Order Volume:** 22K total processed orders.


* 
**Logistics Efficiency:** An average shipping duration of 3.93 days.



---

## 🔍 Business Insights & Segmentation

### Customer & Payment Analytics

* 
**Market Share by Segment:** The **Consumer** segment dominates the portfolio at 51%, followed by Corporate (30%) and Home Office (19%).


* 
**Payment Preferences:** **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the primary payment method at 42%, while Online and Card transactions comprise 37% and 22% respectively.



### Product Portfolio Performance

The inventory is categorized into three primary divisions:

1. 
**Office Supplies:** The highest volume category (0.64M), driven by sub-categories like Storage and Binders.


2. 
**Technology:** Generates 0.47M in sales, with **Phones** serving as the primary revenue driver.


3. 
**Furniture:** Contributes 0.45M, supported significantly by Chair sales.



### Regional & Geographic Distribution

* 
**Primary Market:** The **West Region** leads with 33.37% of total sales.


* 
**Top Performing States:** California (0.34M) and New York (0.19M) represent the core geographic revenue centers.

## 🔮 Predictive Analytics

The second phase of the dashboard utilizes historical time-series data to generate a **15-Day Sales Forecast**.

* 
**Temporal Analysis:** Sales and profit trends are tracked monthly to identify seasonal fluctuations across the 2019-2020 period.


* 
**Forecasting:** The model projects sales volume into **January 2021**, allowing for proactive inventory adjustments and resource allocation.
