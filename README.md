SuperStore Sales Analysis & Forecasting Dashboard📊 Executive SummaryThis project features a comprehensive data visualization suite designed to analyze the retail performance of a North American "SuperStore". By synthesizing transactional data from 2019 and 2020, the dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue streams, regional profitability, and logistics efficiency.📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)The dashboard monitors four critical business metrics to evaluate overall health:Total Revenue: 1.57M in aggregate sales.Net Profit: 175.26K.Order Volume: 22K total processed orders.Logistics Efficiency: An average shipping duration of 3.93 days.🔍 Business Insights & SegmentationCustomer & Payment AnalyticsMarket Share by Segment: The Consumer segment dominates the portfolio at 51%, followed by Corporate (30%) and Home Office (19%).Payment Preferences: Cash on Delivery (COD) is the primary payment method at 42%, while Online and Card transactions comprise 37% and 22% respectively.Product Portfolio PerformanceThe inventory is categorized into three primary divisions:Office Supplies: The highest volume category (0.64M), driven by sub-categories like Storage and Binders.Technology: Generates 0.47M in sales, with Phones serving as the primary revenue driver.Furniture: Contributes 0.45M, supported significantly by Chair sales.Regional & Geographic DistributionPrimary Market: The West Region leads with 33.37% of total sales.Top Performing States: California (0.34M) and New York (0.19M) represent the core geographic revenue centers.🔮 Predictive AnalyticsThe second phase of the dashboard utilizes historical time-series data to generate a 15-Day Sales Forecast.Temporal Analysis: Sales and profit trends are tracked monthly to identify seasonal fluctuations across the 2019-2020 period.Forecasting: The model projects sales volume into January 2021, allowing for proactive inventory adjustments and resource allocation.🛠 Tech Stack & MethodologyData Visualization: Interactive dashboards featuring geospatial mapping and distribution charts.Time Series Modeling: 15-day predictive forecasting for revenue.Data Dimensions: Analysis across Geography, Product Category, Payment Mode, and Shipping Class.
